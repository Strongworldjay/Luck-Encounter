# World Arts and Skill Books

Attack Arts are split into Weapon Arts and Magic Arts. Boost Arts and Passive Arts are separate. The item catalog stores one category per Art family across all its ranks. A Thousand Cuts is Passive; Folkvangr is Weapon (its F rank was already Weapon-only). The inconsistent `Skate FootA` label is now `Skate Foot A` with the same item ID. Flash (Magic) and Smash (Weapon) each have F, D, C, B, A, and S records, mapped to Common through Unique rarity.

The shared item catalog creates one Skill Book for every Art family. For example, `Weapon Art Skill Book: Smash`, `Magic Art Skill Book: Flash`, and `Passive Art Skill Book: A Thousand Cuts` each appear once even though their Arts have six ranks. Books are Uncommon except for single C-rank or rankless Rare Arts, such as the resistances; those books are Rare. Rankless Very Rare Recycle remains an Uncommon Skill Book under this rule. Skill Books carry the source Art's creature and theme tags plus Paper, and record which Art they teach. Reading a book takes 10 minutes and consumes it; if no matching Art slot is free, the reader may replace a known Art.

When adding more Arts, place rank variants in `src/data/items/catalog.json` with one consistent `WeaponArt`, `MagicArt`, `BoostArt`, or `PassiveArt` category for every rank of the family. Names ending in a space and F, D, C, B, A, or S are grouped under the preceding name. `src/data/items/skillBooks.js` creates the corresponding book automatically; no second book dataset is maintained. The data audit rejects duplicate item names, IDs, invalid categories, and missing tags.

Skill Books are in the shop category picker and Magic Shop preset. Direct World Arts, mana, stamina, skill points, and experience cannot be selected or generated by a shop. Every chest type has an additional 5% Skill Book bonus chance, independent of chest tier and creature/theme filters; if selected, the bonus is 95% Uncommon and 5% Rare, adjusted to an available book rarity if necessary. Existing Potion, Ammunition, Scroll, and Gem bonus chances are unchanged. Monster Crystals can yield Skill Books using their normal destruction, rarity, and type matching rules, but cannot yield direct Arts or resources. Dungeon completion cards can yield direct Arts but exclude Skill Books.
